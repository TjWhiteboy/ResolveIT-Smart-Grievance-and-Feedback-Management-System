Frequently Asked Questions

How do I file a complaint?
Click "File New Complaint" in the sidebar or the dashboard button. Fill out the form with a clear title, detailed description, category, and priority. You can also attach supporting documents. Once submitted, your complaint will be assigned a tracking ID.
How do I track my complaint status?
Go to your Dashboard and click on any complaint in the list. The detail page shows a visual timeline of your complaint's progress through each stage: New → Under Review → In Progress → Resolved.
How do I change my password?
Navigate to My Profile → Security tab. Enter your current password, then type your new password and confirm it. Click "Update Password" to save changes.
How do I update my profile picture?
Go to My Profile and click the camera icon over your avatar. Select an image file (JPG/PNG, max 5MB). Your new picture will appear across all pages after upload.
How do I contact admin?
Click "Contact Admin" in the sidebar under Support. Fill out the message form with your subject, details, and priority level. The admin team will review your message and respond accordingly.
How do I download my complaint history?
On your Dashboard, look for the "Export CSV" button near the complaint list. Clicking it will download a spreadsheet of all your complaints including their status, dates, and categories.
